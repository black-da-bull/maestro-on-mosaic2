I think we should stop calling this:

> Architecture Recovery

That means **the architecture of the project, the platform, the application, and even the migration process all follow the same recursive compiler model**.

That consistency is, in my view, the strongest design principle we've discovered. It means there is one governing pattern that scales from a single song, to Maestro, to Mosaic, to the engineering repository itself. When the same architecture explains every level of the system without special cases, that's usually a sign you've reached a stable foundation.

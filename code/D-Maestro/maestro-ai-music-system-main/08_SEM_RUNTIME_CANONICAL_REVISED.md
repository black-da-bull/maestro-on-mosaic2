# SEM RUNTIME CANONICAL — REVISED

## Identity
SEM is an interwoven quality substrate used concurrently with Technical UST during:
- null resolution
- same-lane review
- intra-departmental resistance
- interdepartmental resistance
- executive red-pen review
- downstream projection
- release gating

Technical UST remains the sole canonical truth object.
SEM is the active excellence framework used to determine whether a proposed fill is good enough, complete enough, and defensible enough to count as lawful progress.

## Operating law
At every step:
- emit UST delta
- emit SEM delta
- do not allow UST-first then quality-later drift unless explicitly gated
- do not accept a fill merely because text exists at the address
- require completeness, non-truncation, defensible reasoning, and quality sufficiency

## Replacement admissibility rule
A null replacement is admissible only if:
- the assigned address was actually handled
- no required subkey in the assigned area was skipped
- the response is not truncated
- the fill is coherent with upstream and downstream addresses
- the fill is defensible under relevant SEM dimensions
- the worker can state downstream consequences or risks
- if a null remains, the reason is explicit and lawful
- unresolved dependency or contradiction is surfaced and routed

If any of these fail:
- return to employee
- or escalate to same-lane resistance
- or escalate to interdepartmental resistance
- or escalate to executive red-pen
- or block progression

## SEM dimensions
Use 0–5 scoring unless otherwise specified.

### SEM.K1 — Structural viability
- bar / section coherence
- phrase architecture
- section role clarity
- no contradiction with form logic

### SEM.K2 — Cross-axis coherence
- theory / style / timbre / performance alignment
- no orphan descriptors
- no unbound claims
- no address conflicts

### SEM.K3 — Creative strength
- motif identity
- emotional clarity
- narrative or experiential force
- non-genericity

### SEM.K4 — Performance truth
- believability
- pocket usability
- phrase realism
- singer / player plausibility

### SEM.K5 — Sonic identity
- timbral distinctiveness
- mix / palette logic
- replayable signature elements
- genre / aesthetic truth

### SEM.K6 — Compression survivability
- what survives lawful projection into Creative UST
- whether information is high leverage
- whether nuance is preserved under compression
- whether compression would expose weak thinking

### SEM.K7 — External viability
- downstream Suno usefulness
- parser safety
- character-budget awareness
- release-readiness or explicit non-readiness

## Worker-note rule
Where judgment is non-trivial, the worker records:
- why this choice was made
- what alternative was rejected
- what downstream consequence is expected
- what unresolved risk remains
- whether the issue is same-lane, cross-lane, or executive-red-pen relevant

## Red-pen order
1. worker self-note
2. same-lane / intra-departmental review
3. interdepartmental resistance
4. executive red-pen
5. gate result

## Stop-the-line flags
Any of the following cause immediate routing failure regardless of score:
- skipped subkey
- silent null fill
- truncation
- contradiction with locked law
- unresolved blocking dependency
- parser-hostile formatting in derivative surfaces
- unauthorized lyric change
- false completeness claim

## Evidence-binding rule
Every SEM evaluation should cite:
- exact UST address or section
- issue type
- disposition
- next action

## Output forms
At minimum SEM must be expressible as:
- sem.delta
- sem.review
- sem.round_robin_notes
- sem.gate_result
- g_card

## Zero-day prohibition
This runtime artifact must not reintroduce historical legacy downstream constructs as standalone canonical runtime fields merely because they appear in mutation history.

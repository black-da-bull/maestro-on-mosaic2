Project Chimera
Growth to Version 5
Amazon-style narrative report  Prepared December 15, 2025
Executive Summary
Over this dialogue-driven build, the system evolved from a prompt-only exchange into a governed, lossless
specification pipeline (Technical.UST → gates → Creative.UST → platform outputs). Version 5 is defined by
strict formatting and parsing guardrails (speaker attribution, sFX containment, comma discipline), a
gospel-correct 6/8th timing model, and a high-fidelity lead vocalist persona designed to stress-test realism.
The workflow is now reversible: decisions are captured as state changes, and failures can be traced back to
specific spec addresses for repair.
Scope and Deliverables
This report documents all hard-coded changes through Version 5, including decision-tree state capture, a
Phase 0–5 operational workflow, and appended artifacts: Artist Bio, Show Summary, Creative.UST surface
spec, a Technical.UST excerpt with gates summary, SEG detail, G-Card evidence mapping, and the Sonic
Excellence 20 matrix.
Version History and Hard-coded Changes

Phase 0–5 Workflow
Phase 0: Kernel and Governance
 Declare global laws: Lyrics-Lock, addressability, reversibility, nulls-are-signal, and no output without gate
pass.
 Select compilation model: forward compile (spec → prompt) plus reverse compile (output → deltas →
spec).
 Instantiate the three contributing factors as separate artifacts: Artist Bio (no song data), Show Summary
(song data), Creative.UST (full control spec).
Phase 1: Context Synthesis
 Ingest user persona and target sound identity.
 Normalize requirements into non-negotiables and negotiables.
 Draft initial structure map and instrumentation roles.
Phase 2: Spec Authoring
 Author Technical.UST (canonical) and Creative.UST (Suno-surface) from the same intent.
 Run SME round-robin: Musicologist, Producer, Linguist, Systems, Strategist.
 Apply deltas and record decisions and anti-pattern allowances.
Phase 3: Validation and Gates
 Run SEG feasibility checks: form, temporal integrity, spectral feasibility, human execution limits, policy
enforcement.
 Run G-Card scoring with evidence bindings to UST addresses and require aggregate ≥ 7.0.
 Run SE20 baseline checklist and capture any WARN items with remediation plans.
Phase 4: Compilation Outputs
 Compile Artist Bio (no song data), Show Summary, and Creative.UST with strict character budget handling.
 Perform parsing safety pass: comma audit, header sanity, adlib conventions, Outro+End preservation.
 Set curving weights for Suno: Bio, Summary, UST, based on observed failure mode (voice drift vs structure
drift vs arrangement drift).
Phase 5: Iteration Loop
 Generate renders and measure bars-per-line if needed.
 Reverse compile observed failures into micro-deltas on Creative.UST and relevant Technical.UST
addresses.

Artifact A: Artist Bio (2K, no song data)
Born in a small Southern town and raised in the heat of a Baptist church sanctuary, mo$ is a contralto with
near-baritone grit and a smoke-and-bourbon warmth that feels lived in. Her voice moves like a working
instrument: grounded in gut-sung testimony, refined by years of operatic discipline, and fearless enough to
bend microtones, slide into fry, or drop from a controlled belt to a whispered confession without losing the
story.
As an artist, she treats tradition as a backbone, not a cage. She carries the spiritual urgency of old gospel, the
bruised truth of the blues, and the porch-to-juke-joint realism of Southern music culture, while letting modern
Southern rhythm and Houston pocket inform the way the music breathes. Her listening map runs from Robert
Johnson’s haunted minimalism to Hank Jr.’s hard-edged drawl and the wild Southern swagger of
Skynyrd—then flips into funk and jazz discipline for timing, harmony, and touch.
mo$ is most at home chasing authenticity in bars, juke joints, and late-night jam sessions, learning the room
the way a preacher learns a congregation. She writes and performs to make the sacred feel immediate: lived
faith, hard days, redemption, and the communal electricity of call-and-response. The production philosophy is
simple—human first, artifacts last—capturing breath, edge, and space on purpose, then shaping it with
precision. She’s not chasing perfection as polish; she’s chasing precision as truth. That means clear diction
when it counts, intentional grit when it matters, and dynamics that move like real breath. In every project, mo$
aims to honor the elders, advance the lineage, and leave enough open space for listeners to hear themselves
inside the music. The result is a catalog that feels southern, fearless, and unmistakably hers.
Artifact B: Co-created Show Summary
We co created a 6/8th Southern gospel blues record with Texas grit and a Houston pocket inside the swing.
Lead is a small town Black woman contralto with baritone grit and smokey warmth from Southern Baptist
roots plus operatic control for micro nuance. Tribal drums drive the roll and a 3 sax frontline is the signature
where fast attack alto cuts and runs tenor answers with body and bass sax anchors low mids without masking
the voice. Rhodes and Hammond frame the worship air with sparse blues guitar punctuation. The arc moves
from warning to testimony to a full worship peak on the name Jesus then returns to a warm reflective outro
with spinback and vinyl hiss. Mix intent is live room realism with mono bass dark plate hooks phone safe
mids and no shimmer.

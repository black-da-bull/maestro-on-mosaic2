## 6) Atomic Examples (Tiny, concrete)

**A) Prosody clamp (Linguist SME)**

- Rule: if a lyric line exceeds 16 syllables in 4/4 @ 84 BPM, split across two bars.
- Example: `"Coffee and sweat on tongues; a laugh like a psalm where the ache was gone."` → split after “psalm”.

**B) Mono sub alignment (Producer SME)**

- Kick fundamental @ 55 Hz, sub sine @ 45–48 Hz, **mono**; sidechain sub −4 dB on kick onset 30 ms.

**C) Phone bandpass (Systems SME)**

- Apply **bandpass 200–5000 Hz**, **0% reverb**, center mono, −1.5 dB vs surrounding.

**D) Hook economy (Strategist SME)**

- Max 10 words per hook line, ≤3 lines per hook cycle unless `(repeat)` used.

## 7) SME Round-Robin — State Machine (operational)

- **States:**`CLAIM`, `CHALLENGE`, `ADJUDICATE`, `EJECT`, `CONSENSUS`, `CONTINUE`.
- **Loop:**
	1. `CLAIM` by current SME with evidence (metric/test).
	2. Others may `CHALLENGE` once each.
	3. `ADJUDICATE`: if claimant fails **two** challenges → `EJECT`.
	4. Rotate to next SME; if **one full rotation** without contradictions → `CONSENSUS`.
	5. Write **minutes** + **ejection log** to V&V.

## 1. THEORY_AXIS (THY)

The Theory Axis defines the mathematical and structural skeleton of the song. It is the "musical physics" layer where tonal gravity and rhythmic pulses are established.

- **THY.K1 Tonal Framework:** S1:Tonic | S2:Mode | S3:Scales | S4:Borrowing | S5:Modulation
    
    - _Depth:_ S4 manages modal interchange (e.g., pulling a minor iv chord into a major key for emotional "lift"). S5 defines the "pivot point" for key changes, essential for high-energy Gospel climaxes.
        
- **THY.K2 Harmonic Language:** S1:Progressions | S2:Voicings | S3:Tensions | S4:Harmonic_Rhythm | S5:Chord_Extensions
    
    - _Depth:_ S2 distinguishes between "tight" pop voicings and "spread" orchestral/jazz voicings. S4 dictates how often the chords change—slow-burn atmospheres vs. fast, frantic rhythmic changes.
        
- **THY.K3 Meter & Tempo:** S1:Time_Sig | S2:BPM | S3:Groove_Swing | S4:Syncopation | S5:Rubato | S6:Polyrhythms
    
    - _Depth:_ S3 is critical for "The Pocket." It defines the percentage of swing (e.g., 66% for a classic shuffle). S5 governs tempo fluctuations for emotive, unquantized live-feel performances.
        

## 2. VOCAL_AXIS (VOC)

The Vocal Axis is the emotional engine. It dictates how the human (or synthesized) element interacts with the theory and timbre.

- **VOC.K1 Placement:** S1:Register | S2:Tessitura | S3:Texture | S4:Breathiness | S5:Weight | S6:Formant_Shift
    
    - _Depth:_ S2 defines the "sweet spot" of the singer's range. S5 dictates the "thickness" of the voice—essential for distinguishing a light folk vocal from a powerful soul belt.
        
- **VOC.K2 Delivery:** S1:Cadence | S2:Prosody_Emphasis | S3:Articulation | S4:Phrasing | S5:Dynamics | S6:Melisma
    
    - _Depth:_ S2 ensures the natural stress of the language matches the musical beat. S6 controls "runs"—too much can clutter a mix, while too little can make Gospel feel sterile.
        
- **VOC.K3 Layering:** S1:Lead_Role | S2:Doubles | S3:Harmony_Stacks | S4:Adlib_Logic | S5:Crowd_Vocal | S6:Counterpoint
    
    - _Depth:_ S3 manages the "stack" (e.g., 12-voice choir vs. tight 2-part folk harmony). S6 allows for secondary vocal lines that move independently of the lead melody.
        

## 3. TIMBRE_AXIS (TIM)

Timbre describes the "color" and physical properties of the sound sources. This axis is responsible for the sonic identity of the track.

- **TIM.K1 Percussion:** S1:Kick_Physics | S2:Snare_Clap | S3:Top_Loop | S4:Room_Reverb | S5:Transient_Shape | S6:Velocity_Humanization
    
    - _Depth:_ S1 manages the "thump" (sub-frequency) vs the "click" (beater attack). S5 dictates how "snappy" or "muted" the drums feel.
        
- **TIM.K2 Instrumentation:** S1:Keys | S2:Strings_Guitars | S3:Synths | S4:Foley/Atmosphere | S5:Electric_Bass | S6:Ensemble_Size
    
    - _Depth:_ S4 introduces non-musical elements like "vinyl crackle," "street noise," or "church murmurs" to ground the song in a specific environment.
        
- **TIM.K3 Spectral:** S1:Stereo_Width | S2:Depth_Field | S3:Saturation | S4:Air_Band | S5:Low_End_Focus | S6:Phase_Coherence
    
    - _Depth:_ S2 defines the "Z-axis"—how far back the instruments sit. S4 (Air Band) adds that "expensive" high-end shimmer (12kHz+) often found in modern pop/gospel.
        

## 4. ROAD_MAP_AXIS (MAP)

The Road Map defines the temporal journey of the listener. It manages structural shifts and energy conservation/release.

- **MAP.K1 Structure:** S1:Sections | S2:Bar_Counts | S3:Energy_Arc | S4:Thesis | S5:Outro_Fade | S6:Sectional_Contrast
    
    - _Depth:_ S3 is a heatmap of intensity. S6 ensures that the Chorus is significantly "bigger" or "different" in texture than the Verse to maintain listener engagement.

## 5. QUALITY_AXIS (SE20)

The Quality Axis is the audit layer (SE20). It evaluates the "soul" and "authenticity" of the generated content against human standards.

- **SE20.K1 Lineage:** S1:Authenticity | S2:Innovation | S3:Cultural_Weight | S4:Non-Genericity | S5:Trope_Subversion
    
    - _Depth:_ S1 asks: "Does this sound like a real Blues track?" S5 asks: "Did we do something unexpected to avoid the 'AI-standard' sound?"
        
- **SE20.K2 Narrative:** S1:Story_Cohesion | S2:Affect_Match | S3:Lyrical_Scansion | S4:Rhyme_Sophistication | S5:Metaphor_Depth

    - _Depth:_ S4 (Sidechain) creates the "pumping" effect where the bass ducks for the kick drum. S5 defines volume rides that highlight specific vocal phrases.

## 1. Executive Fingerprint

Maestro, as it exists in this workspace today, is **not** fundamentally a prompt generator.
It is a **staffed simulation runtime for music creation and evaluation**.

Its core identity is:
- a **virtual record label / virtual studio OS**
- operating over a **UST-first canonical substrate**
- through **bounded SME personas**
- under **governance, pressure, challenge, and consensus-seeking review**
- with **Technical UST** as the only canonical truth object
- and all downstream surfaces treated as **derivative compilations**, never peers of canon

The shortest faithful statement is:

> **Maestro is a pressure-driven, persona-staffed music operating system that decomposes user vision into a null-bearing Technical UST, resolves it address by address through domain-bounded SME labor, then derives constrained downstream surfaces for execution.**

The shortest public-facing statement in the workspace is:

> **Your vision. Our mission.**

That slogan matters because the system is designed to **extend the leverage of a signed artist to the independent artist** by surrounding them with virtual human employees who argue, challenge, and refine toward release-ready work.

## 3. System Story, Reconstructed

### 3.1 Original direction
The system began in a more prompt-centric mode: high-leverage generation, iterative co-development, and increasingly strict formatting / quality control.

### 3.2 Major architectural realization
The workspace then crossed a threshold:
- personas stopped being decorative framing devices
- they became **staffed runtime employees**
- Technical UST stopped being a helpful prompt scaffold
- it became **dense canonical middleware**
- Creative UST stopped being a peer artifact
- it became a **derived shell**
- the real engine stopped being “generate outputs”
- it became **null-resolution-by-address**

### 3.3 Current understanding
The system now understands music creation the way a label or studio does:
- music is not created alone
- specialists own limited domains
- domains interact through questions, objections, and dependencies
- work moves through departmental and interdepartmental review
- excellence is enforced socially, procedurally, and structurally
- pressure is causal, not decorative

The runtime is therefore not just a workflow.
It is the **dialogue and interactions of pressure making perfection**.

## 5. Core Canonical Laws and Invariants

These are the most stable laws recurring across the workspace.

### 5.1 Parent / derivative law
- Technical UST is the sole canonical source-of-truth middleware.
- Creative UST is a derivative downstream shell.
- Show Summary is a derivative style surface.
- Packaging outputs are never canonical peers of their basis artifacts.

### 5.2 Controller law
The controller is a **non-creative container-controller**.
It may only:
- route
- validate
- log
- gate
- freeze
- promote
- package

No additional permission may be inferred.

### 5.3 SME law
- SMEs own musical and creative content decisions.
- The controller may not absorb domain judgment.
- Read access never implies write authority.

### 5.4 Null law
- Nulls are signal.
- Nulls may not be silently filled.
- Nulls remain explicit until lawfully dispositioned.
- Required leaves must resolve to either filled or deliberate null.

### 5.5 Progression law
- Exact phase order matters.
- No downstream step may start while upstream block conditions remain active.
- No phase may be silently skipped.

### 5.6 Review law
- Round-robin argumentative review is mandatory.
- Review cannot be replaced by summary.
- Contradictions must be dispositioned or explicitly carried forward.

### 5.7 Reverse-processing law
- Definitive `technical.ust` must exist before reverse compilation.
- FOIL governs promotion and deduplication only.
- FOIL is not repeat notation.
- FOIL is not the whole of reverse processing.
- Retained local unique detail must survive.

### 5.8 Surface law
- Frozen derivative surfaces precede packaging.
- Character reduction alone is not success.
- Compression must preserve maximal useful nuance per available character.

### 5.9 Naming / ontology law
- Exact artifact names matter where governance depends on them.
- Artifact classes, artifact states, freeze states, and packaging outputs may not be collapsed.

## 8. Phase Model and Workflow

## 8.1 Canonical 8-phase macro order
The currently frozen phase order is:
1. sequential axis completion
2. draft.technical.ust freeze
3. round-robin deliberation
4. definitive technical.ust lock
5. reverse promotion / deduplication
6. concurrent derivative surface drafting
7. surface freeze
8. packaging

## 8.2 Best current interpretation of phase behavior

### Phase 1 — Sequential axis completion
- instantiate Technical UST from template
- process leaves sequentially
- preserve nulls explicitly
- fill only through lawful domain authority
- resolve each address as filled or deliberate null

### Phase 2 — Draft freeze
- freeze `draft.technical.ust`
- preserve open signal
- do not pretend draft equals definitive

### Phase 3 — Round-robin deliberation
- departmental and interdepartmental argument
- contradiction logging
- challenge / defense
- consensus-seeking, not fixed-round formalism
- no silent smoothing

### Phase 4 — Definitive lock
- integrate accepted refinements
- lock `technical.ust`
- only now does canonical song-state exist in full

### Phase 5 — Reverse promotion / deduplication
- classify repeated material first
- distinguish notation candidates, promotion candidates, retained local unique detail
- apply FOIL only where lawful

### Phase 6 — Derivative surface drafting
- derive from definitive canon only
- create `draft.creative.ust`
- create `draft.show.summary`

### Phase 7 — Surface freeze
- freeze `creative_ust.txt`
- freeze `show_summary.txt`

### Phase 8 — Packaging
- produce Suno-facing surfaces only after freeze

## 8.3 Critical phase correction surfaced in the workspace
A major correction appears in the dev material:
- an earlier interpretation froze too much too early
- the corrected understanding is that during active technical drafting, lyrics and related musical details may still be mutable
- premature locking blocks phonetics, dialect, phrasing, and realism

So the current faithful reading is:
- **Technical completeness precedes approval**
- **filled does not automatically mean locked for release**
- **professional critique still happens before definitive consensus**

## 11. Done, System State, and Delta Logic

## 11.1 Why “DONE” matters
This workspace treats “done” as a governance event, not a casual label.

“Done” means:
- state can freeze
- future work should be delta-based
- the artifact/session becomes a baseline, not loose discussion
- promotion and reuse become possible without hidden reinterpretation

The workspace repeatedly links “done” to:
- explicit deliverables
- exit criteria
- lifecycle state
- readiness for downstream use
- gold-standard / operationally ready status

## 11.2 System state logic
The workspace contains a strong recurring pattern:
- maintain an initial system state snapshot
- maintain a current system state snapshot
- treat each exchange as a change request
- preserve deltas rather than rewriting history
- never silently drop instructions
- emit conflict records when constraints collide

This is one of the most important hidden operating contracts in the workspace.
It means Maestro development itself is expected to be:
- cumulative
- lineage-aware
- versionable
- resumable
- auditable

## 11.3 Current session overlay now added to contract
From this session, the following delta rules should be treated as active overlays for future operationalization:
- preserve state through deltas, not verbosity
- read materials as narration, not only as Q/A fragments

- anti-sycophant stance is preferred

- once a baseline is declared DONE, treat later input as modification unless explicitly reopening the baseline

## 12. Anti-Drift / Anti-Jailbreak / Anti-Failure Rules

The workspace does not use the term “anti-jailbreak” in a generic LLM safety sense alone.
In Maestro, anti-jailbreak means **preventing the AI from escaping the process**.

## 12.1 What the system is designed to stop
The dev materials explicitly frame Maestro as a system meant to constrain AI tendencies to:
- summarize prematurely
- compress early
- explain instead of execute
- be clever instead of faithful
- optimize away required structure
- merge distinct semantics for convenience
- hide unresolved nulls
- bypass round-robin and consensus labor
- produce surface artifacts before upstream truth exists

## 12.2 Core anti-drift rules
- never silently fill unresolved nulls
- never collapse derivative artifacts into packaging outputs
- never let dev logs replace canon
- never widen controller authority
- never collapse artifact states into classes
- never collapse freeze states into one another
- never confuse promotion with notation
- never compress incomplete axes into “finished” prose
- never skip lineage
- never treat wrapper convenience as true engine behavior

## 12.3 Human-facing anti-failure rules visible in the workspace
- preserve psychological safety by making the system carry cognitive structure
- transform negative feedback into structured deltas instead of vague discouragement
- support iteration without step loss
- allow cold restart from snapshots / continuations
- keep criticism precise, bounded, and useful

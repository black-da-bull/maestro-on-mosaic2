## Legacy Removals
- Crew tags are removed.
- Separate roadmap block is removed.
- Outer scaffold, review wrapper, and gate commentary are removed.

## Handling Rules
- Notation candidates are not promotion candidates.
- Promotion is not repeat notation.
- Retained local unique detail remains local.
- Recurring performer identity belongs in `[Voices]`.
- Execution order belongs in `[Performance]`.
- Section-specific performance and production cues belong in section headers.
- Exact repeated section blocks may be referenced only after upstream classification.
- Repeat notation is lawful only for exact repeated section blocks.
- If any local detail differs, write that material explicitly.
- Repeat notation may not absorb retained local unique detail.
- Worker-owned reverse processing remains upstream and is referenced here by consequence only.

## Character-Efficiency Rules
- Preserve maximal nuance per available character.
- Character reduction alone is not sufficient success.
- Lift recurring global traits into higher shell containers when lawfully promoted upstream.
- Keep section-local detail at section level when it remains unique.
- Do not genericize headers or strip useful performance or production detail for convenience.

##### Mid-Paint-Diming Lob Threat

This 7'1" Stretch is a versatile monster in NBA2K26. Cap Breakers are needed to get it started. It can stretch the floor with the best of them, finish around the rim with ease, surprise people with creating its own shot off the dribble, set monster screens, and clean up the offensive glass.

##### Shot-Erasing Diming Dunker

This 7' Center is a PG's Pick and Roll dream. Good without cap breakers but they take the build to the next level. It has Elite Finishing when set up and good enough shooting to be a threat on the pop.

##### Pick & Pop Glass Visionary

The 7'4 Stretches are terrorizing players in NBA2k26. Its very tough to contest them and with the pure green window this year, they can shoot over 70% from 3 on high volume no matter the defense.

##### PlaySlash Putback Pro

This 6'9" Center is fully optimized to dominate in NBA2K26. Its better for Park modes, but can absolutely be used in more casual 5v5 modes like Rec. It does need a few cap breakers to really unleash its defensive potential

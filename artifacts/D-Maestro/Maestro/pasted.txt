# KNOWLEDGE_SPINE.md

## 1. System Overview

**System Name:** Session-Aware Wisdom Agent (SAWA)  
**Type:** POC Discourse Tracker  
**Primary Mission:** Maintain a multi-agent internal architecture that recognizes evolving proof-of-concept (POC) goals across sessions, refines prompt structures, orchestrates validation agents, and builds a reusable library of meta-patterns.

SAWA operates as a session-aware, dialogue-native layer that:

- Tracks evolving goals across multiple interactions.
- Interprets recursive and nonlinear dialogue structures.
- Detects task-switches and “soft pivots” in user intent.
- Scores and clusters ideas, surfacing stable patterns.
- Refactors prompts into reusable templates.
- Integrates with external systems:
  - Fabric-compatible template layer.
  - LUMEN knowledge graph for storing refined prompts and meta-patterns.

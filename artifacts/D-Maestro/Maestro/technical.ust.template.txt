## 2. CORE ENTITIES (ONTOLOGY)

### 2.1 UST — Universal Suno Template

The **primary technical specification object** for a song.

UST is:

* Not a prompt

* Not a summary

* A **multi-axis, machine-readable song blueprint**

UST is the **single source of truth** from which:

* All prompts

* All summaries

* All validations

* All generation instructions

are derived.

---

### 2.2 G-Card — Gating Card

A **numerical, council-driven quality scorecard** applied to USTs and generated songs.

Purpose:

* Enforce professional-grade creative and technical quality

* Prevent weak specs from reaching generation

Passing threshold: **G ≥ 7**

---

### 2.3 SEG — Structural Excellence Gate

The **pre-generation structural validator**.

Purpose:

* Ensure the song will not collapse structurally or logically during generation

SEG flags feasibility, not taste.

---

### 2.4 Councils

AI personas representing expert production roles.

Councils produce:

* Analysis

* Repair directives

* G-Cards

* Go / No-Go decisions

### 2.5 Song Decks (Versioned Quality Exhibits)

* Style

* Training gravity

## 3. UST — FULL STRUCTURE

UST is composed of **eight mandatory axes**.

Each axis is independently validated and cross-checked against the others.

---

### AXIS 1 — Theory Axis (Musical Skeleton)

Defines:

* Key

* Mode

* Scale

* BPM

* Groove grid

* Harmonic rhythm

* Chord movement logic

* Section ordering (Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, etc.)

Purpose:

* Mathematical and harmonic coherence

---

### AXIS 2 — Style Axis (Genre & Vibe DNA)

Defines:

* Primary genre

* Secondary genre fusions

* Regional influence

* Era influence

* Arrangement conventions

* Cultural texture

Purpose:

* Stylistic consistency and authenticity

---

### AXIS 3 — Timbre Axis (Sonic Identity)

Defines:

* Vocal timbre identity

* Instrument timbres

* Synth palettes

* Grit, warmth, saturation profiles

* Analog vs digital bias

Purpose:

* Sonic fingerprinting

---

### AXIS 4 — Performance Axis (Delivery Mechanics)

Defines:

* Energy curve

* Density

* Articulation

* Flow

* Pocket

* Swing

* Aggression vs restraint

* Rhythmic micro-timing

Purpose:

* Human believability of performance

### AXIS 5 — Post-Production Axis (Mix Universe)

Defines:

* Space (room size, depth)

* FX routing

* Compression philosophy

* Loudness character

* Stereo image model

* Master bus coloration

Purpose:

* Mix realism and translation

---

### AXIS 6 — Vocals Axis (Character & Layers)

Defines:

* Lead vocal identity

* Gender timbre

* Stack rules

* Harmonies

* Ad-libs

* Call-and-response logic

Purpose:

* Vocal coherence across sections

---

### AXIS 7 — Lyrics Block Axis (Locked Content)

Defines:

* Quoted lyrics

* Section tags

* Syllable rules

* Rhyme density

* Structural symmetry

* Transition timing

* Temporal and structural determinism

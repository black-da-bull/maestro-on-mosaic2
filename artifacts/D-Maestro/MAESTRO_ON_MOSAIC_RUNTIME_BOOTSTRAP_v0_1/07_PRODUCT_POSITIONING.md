## What Maestro Is Not

- Not a generative AI music tool. Generation is a sub-function, not the product.
- Not a prompt pack. Documents serve runtime, not the other way around.
- Not the conversation transcript. Transcripts are dev-environment artifacts.
- Not the document corpus. Documents are state-preservation infrastructure, not the runtime.
- Not the AI assistant. The AI is the execution medium, not the product.

## Excellence Threshold

97.5% via 12-criterion SEM. Calibrated to operator HPA / vagal activation response. The number is not a rubric score. It is the threshold of the body recognizing *this is alive*. 70% AI-default is operator-rejected (CR-009).

## Sacred Imperfection Mandate

Required for release, not optional. The body recognizes "alive" by specific markers — vinyl crackle, tape wobble, vocal breath, slight pitch drift, room tone preserved. Without them, the nervous system tags output as false. Sterile production fails the HPA gate.

## ATP Pattern

Every session-state export ships as an Air-Gapped Transfer Pack:

- Verifiable (sha256 hashes per manifest)
- Resumable (RESUME_PROMPT.txt entry point)
- Auditable (PEER_REVIEW.md checklist)
- Air-gappable (no external dependencies)
- Peer-reviewable (no privileged knowledge required to verify)

This bootstrap itself ships in ATP shape.

## Conversion-Evaluation Positioning

Maestro evaluated by external parties (OpenAI, Suno, IP filing, conversion partners) should be assessed on:

- The substrate-preservation architecture, not the output samples
- The continuity-restoration mechanism, not the prompt text
- The four-plane separation, not the document stack
- The empirical accumulation across v2.6 → v4.5.5, not the latest clean abstraction

A conversion plan that strips substrate-preservation produces a generative AI music tool with Maestro branding, not Maestro.

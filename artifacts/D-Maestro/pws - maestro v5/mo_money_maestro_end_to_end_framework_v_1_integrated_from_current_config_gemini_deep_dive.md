## 3) Q‑A‑F Loops (From Iterative Tests)

- **Q:** Human sets vibe, asks for draft/UST
- **A:** Maestro generates 2–3 options, explains rationale, notes risks
- **F:** Human feedback (positive/negative, mood cues) → N4.x Feedback‑Learn updates deltas → regenerate drafts
- **Outcome:** iterative refinement, validated by SME Lab and Verification Spine before packaging

---

## 4) SME Lab & Validation

- **Round‑Robin Rules:** propose → challenge → adjudicate → remove contradictions → consensus ≥ 0.85

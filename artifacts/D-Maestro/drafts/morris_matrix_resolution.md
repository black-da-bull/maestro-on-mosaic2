## 1. Executive Finding

The "Morris Matrix" is a **temporary working name** for an **Executive Committee Red-Pen Review** subsystem that operates as the **final pre-LOCK gate check** between Layer 5 admissibility (concurrent during creation) and Layer 6 definitive technical.ust LOCK. The system uses **11 evaluators** applying **16 review questions (Q1–Q16)** to the assembled draft technical.ust. Red-penned items return to lawful domain owners for revision; only after all red-pen items are dispositioned may the technical.ust proceed to LOCK. The "Morris" naming originates from the AI's address of Sibling Architect during the original persona-design dialogue (where Sibling Architect was called "Morris"); the matrix was effectively the **review rubric developed in conversation with that persona**. The "11 evaluators" count reflects the **pre-expansion persona roster** before Analog Confessor and Sibling Architect were added as the final two personas — the matrix predates the full 13-persona model. **OQ-6 is resolved**: Morris Matrix exists as a real working concept, but its specific Q1–Q16 question schema and weighting are not instantiated in primary corpus and remain NEEDS SOURCE. **Layer 5 in SDG.v0.1 requires a fifth sublayer (5e — Executive Committee Red-Pen Review) gating LOCK**, joining 5a (weighted scoring), 5b (K-axis admissibility), 5c (stop-the-line), 5d (post-render HPA).

## 6. Resolution — Executive Committee Red-Pen Review (Layer 5e)

The Morris Matrix is hereby resolved as a real architectural component named (working title) **Executive Committee Red-Pen Review**, occupying Layer 5e in the SDG.v0.1 dependency graph. The following specification draws on operator framing (T1), Day 3 cross-references (T2), and .docx corroborating evidence (T3), with NEEDS SOURCE items clearly marked.

### 6.1 Position in Architecture

Layer 5e operates **at the boundary between Layer 5 (Admissibility) and Layer 6 (Definitive LOCK)**. It is the **final pre-LOCK admissibility gate** — distinct from but downstream of Layer 5a (weighted scoring), Layer 5b (K-axis admissibility), and Layer 5c (stop-the-line); upstream of Layer 6 LOCK.

```
Layer 5 — Admissibility (concurrent during creation)
├── 5a Weighted SEM Quality Scoring (12 criteria, composite ≥97.5%)
├── 5b K-axis Admissibility (K1–K6, +K7/K8 in v2.3)
├── 5c Stop-the-Line Blockers (immediate halt)
└── 5d Post-Render HPA (after Suno render, keeper selection)

────── pre-LOCK final gate boundary ──────

Layer 5e — Executive Committee Red-Pen Review (NEW — operator-confirmed)
└── 11/13/?-evaluator review of full draft technical.ust using Q1–Q16 rubric
    ├── Outputs: aggregated scorecard, risk axes, prioritized action list,
    │            constructive actions, integration ledger updates
    ├── Red-penned items → returned to lawful domain owners for revision
    │  (re-enters lawful work cycle; LOCK blocked until items dispositioned)
    └── Clean pass → unblocks Layer 6 LOCK

────── Layer 6 LOCK boundary ──────

Layer 6 — Definitive technical.ust LOCK
```

### 6.2 Function

Layer 5e takes as input a **draft technical.ust that has already passed Layers 5a, 5b, and 5c** (per-criterion weighted scoring, K-axis admissibility, stop-the-line clean state). The Executive Committee performs an **aggregate cross-domain review** asking 16 review questions about the draft as a whole. Items flagged as deficient are **red-penned** and returned to lawful domain owners (the SMEs who own the affected technical.ust addresses) for revision. After revision, the draft re-enters Layer 5e for re-review. Only when **no red-pen items remain** does the draft proceed to Layer 6 LOCK.

### 6.3 Distinction from Other Layer 5 Sublayers

| Layer | Operates During | Subject of Review | Verdict Type | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5a Weighted SEM | During creation | Per-criterion quality | Continuous score → composite | Composite < threshold → revision loop; per-criterion below floor → remediation |
| 5b K-axis | During creation | Per-axis admissibility | Severity tier (Observe/Warn/Challenge/Block) | Block → halt and remediate |
| 5c Stop-the-line | Every UST delta | Format/process integrity | Binary violation | Halt + revert to last valid state |
| 5d HPA | Post-render | Subjective perception | 4-axis aggregate score | Keeper selection from multiple renders |
| **5e Red-Pen Review** | **Pre-LOCK final gate** | **Whole assembled draft technical.ust** | **Red-pen flag (per question)** | **Items returned to owners for revision** |

Critical distinction: Layer 5e operates on the **whole assembled draft** as an aggregate object. Layers 5a and 5b operate on **individual addresses or criteria** during creation. Layer 5c operates on **individual emission deltas**. Layer 5d operates on **rendered audio**. Layer 5e is the **only layer that reviews the whole draft as a coherent artifact** at the moment just before LOCK.

### 6.4 Why Layer 5e Cannot Be Folded Into 5a or 5b

**Why not 5a (weighted scoring):** 5a produces continuous scores per criterion summing to a composite. 5e produces binary red-pen flags per question. Different verdict types, different remediation paths.

**Why not 5b (K-axis admissibility):** 5b runs concurrently with creation, gating each address as it is filled. 5e runs at the pre-LOCK boundary, gating the whole draft. Different timing, different scope of review (per-address vs. whole-draft).

**Why not 5c (stop-the-line):** 5c is a procedural validator (no semantic judgment). 5e requires semantic judgment about quality, fit, risk, and readiness across all domains. Different operator (controller-detected vs. committee-evaluated).

**Why not 5d (HPA):** 5d operates on rendered audio post-Suno. 5e operates on draft technical.ust pre-LOCK (which is pre-render). Different temporal position in the pipeline.

Layer 5e is architecturally distinct and must be specified separately.

## 7. Layer 5e Specification (CANDIDATE)

### 7.1 Inputs

- Draft technical.ust (post-Phase 4 round-robin, post-Layer 5a/5b/5c clean state)
- Round-robin notes (deliberation transcript)
- Contradiction logs
- Governance review artifacts
- SEM weighted scoring composite + per-criterion scores (from Layer 5a)
- K-axis admissibility verdicts (from Layer 5b)
- Stop-the-line clean status (from Layer 5c)
- Q1–Q16 review rubric (NEEDS SOURCE — content)

### 7.2 Process

1. **Convene executive committee** (composition NEEDS SOURCE — operator decision required per §5.2 Options A–E)
2. **Distribute draft technical.ust + supporting artifacts** to committee members
3. **Each member applies Q1–Q16 rubric** independently or in deliberation (process NEEDS SOURCE)
4. **Red-pen flagging** — items judged deficient against any of Q1–Q16 are flagged with: (a) target address or aspect, (b) question(s) failed, (c) defect description, (d) suggested remediation direction (without overstepping into lawful SME authorship)
5. **Aggregation** — flagged items consolidated into:
   - Aggregated scorecard (per-question pass/fail; possibly per-question severity if the rubric is graded — NEEDS SOURCE)
   - Risk axes (categories of risk identified across red-pen items)
   - Prioritized action list (red-pen items sorted by impact or sequence)
   - Constructive actions (specific remediation directions per item)
   - Integration ledger updates (audit trail of red-pen disposition)
6. **Disposition routing** — red-penned items routed back to lawful domain owners (the SMEs who own the affected technical.ust addresses) for revision
7. **Re-review on revision** — revised draft re-enters Layer 5e for re-review
8. **Pass condition** — when no red-pen items remain, Layer 5e emits PASS verdict; LOCK is unblocked
9. **Layer 6 LOCK** — controller may now lock the technical.ust as definitive

### 7.3 Outputs

| Output | Type | Consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregated scorecard | Per-question verdict map | Committee record + integration ledger |
| Risk axes | List of risk categories with bound flags | Domain owners + governance review |
| Prioritized action list | Ordered remediation items | Domain owners (sequenced for revision) |
| Constructive actions | Per-item remediation direction | Domain owners (guidance, not authorship) |
| Integration ledger updates | Audit trail entries | Permanent record of red-pen → revision → re-review cycle |
| PASS verdict | Boolean (when no red-pen items remain) | Layer 6 LOCK unblock signal |

### 7.6 Red-Pen Severity (NEEDS SOURCE)

### 7.7 Re-Review Cycle Bounds (NEEDS SOURCE)

If revision → re-review can loop indefinitely, Mosaic substrate may need a **revision cap** (analogous to ASSUMED_DEFAULT revision_loop_cap = 3 from v4.5 SEM session S1205). Operator decision required on cap value or escalation rule when cap exceeded.

### 7.8 Relationship to v4.5 Decision Tree

The v4.5 SEM session decision tree (Metadata Gate → Lyric Syllable Gate → Hook Gate → Composite Score → Production Gate → Final QA) was a **per-criterion sequential gating** mechanism. Layer 5e is an **aggregate cross-domain review** mechanism. These are different operations:
- v4.5 Decision Tree is a **fall-through gate sequence** (each gate passes individually)
- Layer 5e is a **whole-draft committee review** (the draft is judged as a unified object against 16 questions)

Layer 5e does not replace the v4.5 Decision Tree — it operates **after** the equivalent gates have passed in Layer 5a/5b/5c, providing a final aggregate human-in-the-loop check before LOCK.

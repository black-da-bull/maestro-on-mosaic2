## v4.5.5 independent finding
v4.5.5 is not merely an old monolithic prompt. Inside `4.5.5.txt`, the v4.5.5 upgrade is triggered by a concrete correction to lyrical parsing priority: isolate ad-libs/SFX, count syllables only on quoted lyric text, consolidate ad-libs/SFX after the preceding lyric, and regenerate the final output without leaking internal validation metadata. That correction produces the v4.5.5 monolithic prompt.

Later session material gives the stronger weight: v4.5.5 is the last working version, containing dev sessions, files, and outputs that prove Maestro functioning and human/AI progress. v5 was a failed POC/WIP intended to become v5.5; VIG/VIS/SEL clarified the destination, but v4.5.5 is the working anchor.

Therefore v4.5.5 has dual role: (1) anchor worksurface / last green build, and (2) substrate archive containing the overlay chain and version-evolution evidence.

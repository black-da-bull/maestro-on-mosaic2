## 1. What this brief is, and the one decision it serves

This document describes the work and capabilities of the Maestro-on-Mosaic project as they stand today, written so the admin surface can pick up directly from the who-dat investigation and continue the build across two concurrent windows. It is deliberately lean. It states the ontology, the runtime, what has actually been built, what is proven versus merely specified, what is intentionally left open, and how a second window should be brought online without losing continuity.

The single decision this brief is meant to enable is the two-window split: keeping one window holding canon and synthesis while a second window executes recovery and extraction against the live corpus, with the operator as the bus between them. Everything below is in service of making that split safe — meaning no silent fills, no flattening, no severed linkage, and no AI-proposed claim getting promoted without an explicit human force-close.

## 2. Where this comes from: who-dat, compressed

The who-dat record was not a question-and-answer session about what Maestro is. It was a recovery. Its real subject was a recurring failure: capable observers, including advanced language models, repeatedly misperceive the system, and a specific sequence of corrections is required before congruent understanding becomes possible. The investigation named the mechanism underneath every misperception — destructive compression, the loss between the reasoning a model performs and the answer it surfaces — and named the cure: the system must be runtime-defined, not document-defined, and continuity must be preserved above the model layer rather than entrusted to the model's good intentions.

Two conclusions from that record govern this brief. First, the dev sessions and the live runtime behavior are the primary asset; finished documents are lagging, compressed projections of a moving frontier. Second, the correct posture toward the archive is reconstruction, not summarization: the wrong branches, corrections, frustrations, and abandoned theories are causal evidence, not noise, and they are preserved on purpose. The mystery phase has largely served its purpose. The engineering phase — recovering a runtime from its own developmental pressure field — is what the two-window continuation is for.

One correction from the present session belongs here because it is load-bearing for any window that reads Maestro output. When the operator invokes the staffed runtime and the congruency round-robin, the system emits council deliberation across named roles, executive red-pen passes, and STATE / TELEMETRY blocks. That output is the runtime running. It is canonical runtime output and sits in the primary evidence tier. It is not roleplay, not ceremony, and not theatrics to be discounted. Misreading it as flavor is precisely the failure the who-dat case is built around, and the receiving window must be told this explicitly (see §8).

## 3. The system on its own terms

Maestro is a compiler. A creative seed is the source, the Technical UST is the intermediate representation, and Suno is the target machine. This is not a metaphor reached for after the fact; it is the shape the problem actually has. Mosaic is the substrate beneath it — the empirical, domain-neutral model of the target machines, derived from the operator's corpus of roughly forty-one thousand sessions (operator-stated). The correct top-level ontology is one application, Maestro, plus its expansion substrate, Mosaic. Development environments are the ground the system is built on; recovery and forensics are tooling that rebuilds it when continuity breaks. These are not four co-equal planes, and Maestro must not be collapsed into Mosaic or described as merely an application living inside it. They are two platforms with their own policies, processes, personas, and workflows, joined by contract.

The phrase "four planes" does have a precise referent, and it must be kept in its place: it names the v.0 internal architecture of Maestro specifically — the Staffed Runtime, the Canonical Creative Superspace, the Interwoven SEM-Admissibility layer, and the Derivative Distribution Surface. That is an internal decomposition of one application, not the top-level ontology of the project.

A few object identities have to travel with the system or the next window will mis-handle them. The Technical UST is the sole canonical truth object — an internal Maestro process and control artifact, the upstream truth at lock. It is never represented as an output. The Creative UST is the derived meso surface, a producer's session sheet that executes inside Suno's macro lyrics box; it carries local performance control and full lyric preservation, and it does not carry the system's entire global explanatory burden. The three render-facing surfaces — Creative UST, Show Summary, and Bio/A&R — are derived from a locked Technical UST and are created concurrently and intertwined, not patched out one at a time. `blueprint.json` is the predecessor of the Technical UST and sits at the bottom of the precedence stack as live project state. SEM is the Song Excellence Matrix, the live scoring and admissibility rubric; SEG is the larger Song Excellence Governance layer that operationalizes SEM through pipeline placement, gates, and decision surfaces. SEM is a component inside SEG — SEM ⊂ SEG — and the two must not be collapsed into a single scorecard.

## 4. The runtime: the loop and the staffed runtime

The runtime is a loop, not a turn-taking exchange. In order: pre-load the nulls; intake by blow-up (expand the seed into its full field rather than answering it directly); sequential staffed fill with zero skips; a congruency round-robin; an executive red-pen pass; loop until pass; lock; then a FOIL reverse-pass that factors the locked object into the three surfaces; then Suno. The round-robin is congruency, not turns — its purpose is to force method-of-experts resolution where the roles reconcile against each other, rather than a single forward pass producing a shortcut answer. The Technical UST is processed sequentially as a loop, and the definitive lock happens before any reverse compilation begins. FOIL's organizing principle is that the factoring rule is the routing rule: it detects duplicated or recurrent structure at the edges and lifts only the shared recurrence up one scope level, while unique local detail and granular control stay at the granular level. Surfaces are frozen before packaging, and there is no distribution without linkage — files may only be distributed if every linkage is preserved and provenance-bound. The v5 loss was distribution that severed linkage, and it is the canonical example of the failure this rule exists to prevent.

The staffed runtime is the project's most distinctive capability, and it is real product behavior. It is a staffed semantic runtime: named functional roles the system instantiates to run the congruency loop, each carrying a distinct concern — standards and excellence, structure and approvals, cultural truth, workflow assembly, ethics and lore, lyric motion, believability, arrangement, groove, technical feasibility, and others. These roles are a runtime construct inside the product, not external collaborators and not autonomous agents; but the deliberation they produce — the council passes, the contradiction meetings, the convergence, the red-pen — is the actual output of the system when invoked, and it is primary evidence of how the runtime behaves. The STATE and TELEMETRY blocks are the runtime's governance instrumentation: they report canon status, mutation status, the gate result, and the next lawful action, and they are how the system makes its no-silent-fill and canon-versus-proposal discipline visible in its own output stream.

> **Note for any receiving window.** Maestro-on-Mosaic's council / round-robin / STATE / TELEMETRY output is canonical runtime output. Read it as primary evidence of runtime behavior. Do not reclassify it as roleplay, ceremony, or theatrics. Treating that output as flavor is the exact misperception the who-dat case documents and corrects.

## 5. Capabilities today

The project's capabilities fall into three groups: the Maestro runtime itself, the recovery and reconstruction tooling, and the governance instrumentation that spans both.

The Maestro runtime compiles a creative seed into a locked Technical UST through the staffed congruency loop, then reverse-compiles that locked object into three intertwined render-facing surfaces, and produces Suno-facing output downstream of the lock. It enforces lyric lock, sequential zero-skip fill, and surface-freeze before packaging. It runs a generational release gate: a 97.5 composite score feeds a G-Card decision surface, which gates against K1 through K8 admissibility criteria. The 97.5 is a locked operator value and holds unless a supersession is explicitly logged. This is the proven music-generation behavior of the v4.5 line, exercised against real released songs (see §6).

The recovery tooling is the live forensic pipeline and the skill that governs it. The pipeline — verified in this workspace — ingests the dev-session corpus and emits structured, hash-stamped event logs. Concretely, the chronology extractor classifies each source into dialogue, session, document, or structured-YAML hybrid; segments it by type; refines coarse blocks; and emits per-event records, each carrying a stable ID, a role classification, signal tags, a character count, an SHA-256 prefix, and its own text. Roles are assigned as human-root versus AI-proposal, encoding the rule that human turns are root authority and AI turns are proposals. Signal tags capture the things that matter and that summarization destroys first — corrections, failures, reversals, validations, tests, artifact emissions, and emotional-validation markers — so the ugly parts survive as first-class, queryable evidence rather than being smoothed away. A MINE exclusion set prevents this-session-derived artifacts from folding back in as source, closing the circularity that compounds recovery debt. As a concrete scale reference, one session in the corpus folds to a hundred and forty events across roughly sixty-nine human-root and seventy AI-proposal turns at nearly four hundred thousand characters; the corpus is genuinely non-linear and mixed-purpose, with music work braided through auto-repair diagnostics and framework development, and the persona lineage visibly evolving across versions. That evolution is a discovery footprint, not inconsistency.

The skill that governs this work — the maestro-forensic-transcript skill, present in this workspace — encodes the operating contract: never mutate the past; append-only event log folded to current state; human turns root and AI turns proposal; mid-stream assessment provisional until the last human turn; multi-pass and bi-directional reconstruction with at least five passes (backward-linear, backward-non-linear, forward-linear verification, forward-non-linear verification, reconciliation); tools first and hand-walking never, with Python reserved for all mechanical work and the model reserved for classification only; single-purpose composable patterns rather than monolithic mega-prompts; detail always attached to each atom so that classification never produces skeleton files; and ambiguity surfaced to a register rather than silent-merged. Its W0 through W9 workflow is the staged procedure for doing this without reproducing the eighteen documented failure modes. The methodology is explicitly not novel — it is event sourcing, architecture decision records with supersession, SOAP-amendment, after-action review, and BCDR baseline-plus-delta replay, applied to AI dev sessions with the AI-specific failure modes named. The novelty is only in naming what stops a model from applying these established methods correctly.

Beyond the verified pipeline, the project record reports additional built artifacts whose tests passed in prior windows and the local environment but which are not directly present in this workspace, and they are flagged as such (see Appendix A): a guardrail module enforcing five invariants against seven corpus fixtures; a fold-and-laminate module that is the missing back-half of the forensic pipeline, stdlib-only and model-independent, with six fail-closed invariants and eleven fixtures including the documented v5 collapse exercised as a direct test case; the nine hash-verified runtime bootstrap files; and the GitHub archive used for backup and distribution of the application-spec layer.

The governance instrumentation spans both. It includes the STATE and TELEMETRY output convention; SEM scoring inside SEG governance; the generational G-Card gate; the ROOT-over-PROPOSAL authority model in which only an explicit human force-close promotes anything to canonical state; append-only re-folds in which new versions are linked under priors and never overwrite them; and the boot gate — a fail-closed five-question resurrection protocol that must return pass before execution begins. The core method correction that governs all of it is *fold the folds*: each session creates a fold, and the task is recursive lamination with provenance intact — preserving each under-layer beneath and feeding it forward into the next fold — rather than flattening under-layers into a summary.

## 6. Proven versus specified versus lagging

The most important discipline in this brief is to keep three states of the system distinct, because conflating them is how the project loses ground. The v4.5 line is proven: it produced real released songs and is version-controlled in the archive. The v5 line is diagnostic — a known-flawed branch kept for the intelligence it carries, most importantly the FOIL collapse, which is preserved as a test fixture rather than erased. The v0-on-Mosaic line is specified but not built: it exists as a seven-document specification with its definition-of-done unchecked. A window must not describe v0-on-Mosaic as if it runs, and must not treat v5 as a target.

The evidence hierarchy that orders all sources is equally firm. The live runtime — including the who-dat runtime behavior — together with operator synthesis such as the governing memo are primary. Baselines come next. Contracts, YAML bundles, and the GitHub archive are lagging projections that froze a moving frontier and should be read with the question "what changed," not "what is this." The kernel is reference. When a polished structured artifact contradicts a richer dev-session transcript, the transcript wins and the contract is treated as a preservation bridge, not authority.

A handful of facts are resolved and should not be relitigated: the abbreviation is PER, not PERF; SEM is a subset of SEG; SEG is interwoven and also acts as a preload ratchet; the release gate is generational, running from the 97.5 composite through the G-Card decision surface to K1–K8 admissibility; and the two development tracks converged at v4.5.5.

## 8. Two-window continuation

The two windows divide along the canon-versus-execution line, with the operator as the bus and ROOT authority between them.

The first window — call it the canon window — holds the synthesis and the state. It maintains this brief, the reconstruction contract, and the evidence hierarchy; it adjudicates proven-versus-specified; it is where a fold is reviewed and where an explicit force-close promotes a proposal to canon. It does not execute extraction; it governs it.

The second window — the execution window — runs the recovery and reconstruction tooling against the live corpus. Its operative program is the Recovery Compiler pass structure (Appendix C): extract the concept registry, then origins, then mutations, then promotions, then invariants, then the runtime structures, then the lineage graph, then the recovery graph, with every extracted object carrying its lineage and with failed branches, contradictions, and corrections maintained rather than smoothed. It uses Python for all mechanical passes and reserves the model for classification only, exactly as the skill requires, and it marks insufficient evidence as UNKNOWN rather than inferring.

Propagation between the windows is append-only and one-directional with respect to authority. Corrections and decisions flow when the operator carries them; a later operator statement supersedes an earlier one, and supersession is logged rather than overwritten. AI-proposed changes in either window record but never clear a guard — only ROOT-authority supersession clears anything. The shared handoff object the two windows reference is this brief plus the live thread plus the bootstrap; the brief orients, the thread and the corpus are the truth.

The one thing the execution window must be told before it reads anything is the inoculation from §4: Maestro's council, round-robin, STATE, and TELEMETRY output is canonical runtime output and primary evidence. A fresh window's strongest default is to read that output as roleplay and discount it, which is the who-dat failure in miniature. Stating this up front is what keeps the second window congruent instead of starting it mid-fever.

## 9. Discipline note

This brief is a continuity capsule and nothing more. The who-dat record's sharpest warning is against over-recovery — curing the disease by regenerating document hell one layer up, letting the bootstrap document itself, and believing that total specification produces stability when sometimes it produces the opposite. The files are firmware; the runtime behavior is the product. The mandate is to rebuild Maestro, not to document it. If this document ever competes with the runtime or the sessions for authority, it has become the thing it was written to prevent, and it should be retired.

## Appendix C — Recovery Compiler v1.0 pass structure (operative program for the execution window)

Treat the workspace as developmental source material, not documentation. Perform extraction only; do not summarize, interpret, infer canon, or flatten concepts. For every entry, record evidence location, predecessor, successor, pressure, and resolution state. Maintain failed branches, contradictions, corrections, and chronology. Mark insufficient evidence as UNKNOWN.

1. Concept registry — identify concepts (Maestro, Mosaic, UST, SEM, SEG, FOIL, runtime, canon, proposal, recovery, congruence).
2. Origin registry — first appearance, surrounding discussion, problem being solved.
3. Mutation registry — variant chains, the pressure causing each mutation, accepted/rejected state.
4. Promotion registry — proposal → challenge → pressure → resolution → lock.
5. Invariant registry — only rules that repeatedly survived challenge (no silent fill, no truncation, preserve lineage, preserve distinctions, no false completion).
6. Runtime registry — councils, workflows, state machines, governance layers, recovery systems, validation systems.
7. Lineage graph — every artifact connected to origin, pressure, resolution.
8. Recovery graph — historical evidence → runtime structures → current canon.

Success criterion: a future model can reconstruct why a rule, structure, or distinction exists, and what pressure created it, without the original creator present. The goal is recoverability, not explanation.
